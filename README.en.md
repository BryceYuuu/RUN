# RUN · 润

**A relocation handbook for Chinese mainland passport holders**, covering 36 European countries (110 immigration routes) and 13 destinations outside Europe: digital nomad visas, skilled work and the EU Blue Card, study-to-work routes, startup and self-employment routes, passive-income and retirement visas, and residence by investment (golden visas).

- Last updated **3 November 2026**. The European section is reorganised and rewritten from [LetsMigrate.org](https://letsmigrate.org/en/countries/) country pages, programme records and guides, each cross-referenced to official sources (378 official links in `data/programmes.json`).
- The global section (US, Canada, Australia, NZ, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong, Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia, UAE, Latin America) is compiled from government and third-party sources and is less rigorously verified.
- Content is written in Simplified Chinese. Structured data is in [`data/programmes.csv`](data/programmes.csv).

> **Not legal, tax or immigration advice.** Rules change constantly; always verify on official government websites.

## FAQ

### Which European countries offer a digital nomad visa?

The handbook covers 15 European digital nomad routes. Income thresholds start at about €330 a month and vary widely: Spain from €2,442/month, Portugal €3,680/month, Romania about €5,500/month. See [digital nomad visas](docs/routes/digital-nomad.md).

### Which country has the lowest EU Blue Card salary threshold?

Slovakia has one of the lowest in Europe, at EUR 1,944 a month. Across 50+ skilled-work routes, the fastest path to permanent residence takes 21 months. See [skilled work and the EU Blue Card](docs/routes/skilled-work.md).

### Are there still golden visas in Europe?

Yes, but many have closed: Spain stopped accepting golden visa applications in 2025, Malta's citizenship by investment was struck down in 2025, Latvia closed property-based residence in September 2026, and Portugal's golden visa no longer accepts real estate. Greece, Cyprus, Malta and Hungary still have options. See [residence by investment](docs/routes/investment.md).

### Can I stay in Europe to work after studying?

Most countries give graduates 6–36 months to find a job, for example 18 months in Germany and 2 years in Finland or after a master's in Ireland. See [study → work → residence](docs/routes/study-to-work.md).

### Is this only useful for Chinese citizens?

Many routes are open to non-EU nationals in general, and some rules differ by nationality. The document preparation and passport notes are written for Chinese mainland passport holders.
