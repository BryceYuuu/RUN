# RUN · 润

**A relocation handbook for Chinese mainland passport holders**, covering 36 European countries (99 immigration routes) and 13 destinations outside Europe: digital nomad visas, skilled work and the EU Blue Card, startup and self-employment routes, residence by investment.

- Data as of **October 2026**. The European section is reorganised and rewritten from [LetsMigrate.org](https://letsmigrate.org/en/countries/) country pages, programme records and guides, each cross-referenced to official sources (378 official links in `data/programmes.json`).
- The global section (US, Canada, Australia, NZ, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong, Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia, UAE, Latin America) is compiled from government and third-party sources and is less rigorously verified.
- Content is written in Simplified Chinese. Structured data is in [`data/programmes.csv`](data/programmes.csv).

> **Not legal, tax or immigration advice.** Rules change constantly; always verify on official government websites.
